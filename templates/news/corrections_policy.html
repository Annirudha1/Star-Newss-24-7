Corrections Policy

STAR NEWS 24×7 · Publisher: Sunil Sharma

Report a possible error

Email starnewshindimodinagar@gmail.com with the article or video URL, the specific information you believe is incorrect, and a reliable source or supporting detail if available. The publisher contact is also listed on Contact Us.

Review and correction

We review correction requests and, when an error is confirmed, update the affected item as appropriate. Material corrections should be made clearly, and a correction note or updated date should be shown when available. Minor spelling or formatting changes may be made without a separate notice.

Our original reporting

News reports and video coverage on this website are produced by STAR NEWS 24×7. We review reported errors in our own content and update or clarify an item when appropriate. If a correction concerns supporting material or a credited image, include that detail in your request.

We do not promise a response time, but consider requests as promptly as possible.
