Editorial Policy

STAR NEWS 24×7 · Publisher: Sunil Sharma

Our responsibility

STAR NEWS 24×7 publishes original news reporting and video coverage under the responsibility of publisher Sunil Sharma. Our newsroom prepares the reports published on this website. Individual bylines are shown only when supplied by the newsroom.

Accuracy and verification

We seek to check names, dates, locations, and key claims against available information before publication. Breaking reports may develop and be updated. We will correct material errors under our Corrections Policy.

Original content and credits

News reports and video coverage on this website are produced by STAR NEWS 24×7. Where supporting information, media, or image credits apply, we identify them when known. Author names and credits are not guessed. See our Original Content & Attribution Policy.

Independence and transparency

We aim to distinguish news reporting from commentary and to identify sponsored or promotional material if published. Readers may contact the publisher with questions about an item.
